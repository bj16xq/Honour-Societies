## Background
* An honour society having focus groups is an effective way to engage members and support productive conversation that may lead to advocacy and the improvement of programs or policy.
* Theses are often the most important aspect of a research-based program.

**Note that thesis is used to encapsulate theses, dissertations, major research papers, and exegeses (for arts-based or capstone projects).**

## Content

### Discussion 1: "Challenge Mode" Thesis
**Introduction**\
This discussion is meant to facilitate contemplation of a hypothetical initiative called the "Challenge Mode" Thesis.\
Thesis students are provided the option to, upon completing their thesis, write a special draft of their thesis by adding any combination of features that turn the original thesis into a "challenge mode" thesis.\
These features are meant to elevate the experience for the reader through adding challenge and gamification.
Possible features for "challenge mode" are as follows:\
* Vocabulary challenge: replacing a portion of words with low-frequency words (i.e., difficult words) without altering meaning. This generally targets regular words or common academic terms rather than disciplinary terminology.
* Comprehension questions for the reader in one of two forms: (i) after the entire thesis, which is better for shorter theses or exegeses; (ii) after each section/chapter, which is better for longer theses.

**Preliminary Considerations**
* Establishing an institutional policy may prompt the institution to: (a) communicate to thesis students support dissemination of the challenge mode thesis
* Students may always undertake this task in an informal capacity
* One possibility aside from policy students 

**Discussion Questions**
* What are the pros and cons to establishing a policy on the challenge mode thesis?
